How the refined score works:
The model rewards tight spreads, higher volume, higher open interest, reasonable strike distance, and premiums inside your preferred band. Uniformity is calculated first from in-band contracts. If there are too few, the model falls back to all selected contracts. Confidence combines score quality, spread quality, and premium uniformity.
